Pint's Craft Upgrades v3.1 - gear your characters can craft that would be an upgrade
(WoW Classic Era / Hardcore / Forever)

REQUIRES
  Pint's Alts (it knows your characters, their gear and their recipes).
  Optional: Pints Recipe Sources - adds recipes nobody has learned yet that someone could learn.

INSTALL
  Unzip so the folder sits at:  World of Warcraft\_classic_era_\Interface\AddOns\PintsCraftUpgrades
  Restart the game. Log into each character and open its profession windows once.

HOW TO USE
  Opens as the Craft Upgrades tab in Pint's Alts, or on its own: /craftup or the minimap button.
  - Pick any character from the list on the left (starts on the one you're playing each session).
  - Results are grouped by slot: the item, how much better it scores than what's equipped
    (or "empty" for an empty slot), its materials, and who can craft it.
  - Hover a material to see which of your characters have it, and where (bags, bank, mail).
  - Craft for Alts: what the picked character can make that would upgrade your other characters,
    grouped by character. Bind-on-pickup crafts are left out - they can't be handed over.
  - Filters: Known recipes / Learnable now / Learnable soon (within 25 skill) /
    Wearable soon (0-30 levels ahead, set with - and +).
  - Only gear the character can actually use: level, armor type and weapon skills are checked.

SCORING
  Each item is valued with stat weights for the character's role (melee, agility, hunter, tank,
  caster, healer), picked from class and talents. Change it per character:
  /craftup role melee | agile | hunter | tank | caster | healer | auto

COMMANDS
  /craftup         open or close
  /craftup role    show or set the scoring role
