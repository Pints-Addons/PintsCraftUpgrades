Pint's Craft Upgrades - Changelog

v3.2.4
- Gear your class can't use (a wand or bow for a shaman, a shield for a priest, plate for a hunter) is never an
  "upgrade" - in Pint's Quest Book's rewards and everywhere else that asks.

v3.2.3
- WoW Forever: your role comes from your talent trees (read with Pint's Tips) - a Restoration shaman was scored as a
  caster, so spell-damage gear beat healing gear.
- New default: the "wear soon" filter is ticked too. (Your own settings are kept.)

v3.2.2
- WoW Forever: gear with "Increases healing done by up to X and damage done by up to Y" now counts that healing and
  spell damage (it was ignored, so caster and healer gear scored too low).
- WoW Forever: your role follows your talent tree again - the one Pint's Tips shows (a Restoration Shaman scores gear
  as a healer, not melee). The talents weren't being read, so everyone got their class's default role.

v3.2.1
- WoW Forever: characters are known by their full name (first and second, e.g. "Pint Waterboy"), so characters that
  share a first name no longer share one record. Classic Era is unchanged.

v3.2
- Works on WoW Forever (the modern game client). Classic Era is unchanged.
- Uses the modern item, spell and profession functions where the old ones are gone.

v3.1
- First public release as Pint's Craft Upgrades.
- Pick any of your characters (from Pint's Alts), not just the one you're on.
- Craft for Alts: what one character can make that would upgrade the others.
- Docks into Pint's Alts as its Craft Upgrades tab, with your characters down the left.
- Wearable soon now looks 0-30 levels ahead.
